FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before booking your drone photography services shoot — answered clearly and directly.

Q

What types of projects do you cover?

We handle a wide range of aerial projects including real estate drone photography services, commercial property showcases, construction progress documentation, event coverage, film and video production, landscape and environmental surveys, and industrial inspections. If it benefits from a bird's-eye perspective, we can capture it.

Q

How long does it take to receive my footage?

Standard turnaround for edited photos is 48–72 hours after the shoot. Video projects typically deliver within 5–7 business days depending on complexity and length. Rush delivery options are available — just let us know your deadline when booking.

Q

What happens if the weather is bad on shoot day?

Safety and image quality are our top priorities. If conditions include high winds, rain, fog, or restricted visibility, we will reschedule at no additional charge. We monitor forecasts closely and communicate early so your timeline is never left in limbo. Most rescheduled shoots happen within 48 hours.

Q

Do you handle FAA permits and airspace clearances?

Yes. All of our pilots are FAA Part 107 certified and we manage all necessary airspace authorizations, LAANC approvals, and site-specific waivers. You do not need to worry about compliance — we take care of the regulatory side so you can focus on the results.

Q

What should I prepare before the shoot?

Clear the site of vehicles, clutter, or debris that could distract from the shot. If it's a property shoot, ensure lawns are mowed and staging is complete. Share any specific angles, boundaries, or restricted zones with us beforehand. We'll send a pre-shoot checklist once your booking is confirmed.

Q

What areas do you service?

We operate across the region and are available for travel to project sites outside our local area. Travel fees may apply for shoots beyond a standard radius. Contact us with your location and we'll confirm availability and any additional logistics.

Q

What file formats will I receive?

Photos are delivered as high-resolution JPEGs (and RAW files upon request). Video is delivered in MP4 format at 4K resolution unless otherwise specified. All files are shared via a private download link within the agreed delivery window.

Q

How do I book a shoot?

Simply reach out through our contact form or give us a call. We'll discuss your project goals, site details, and timeline, then send you a quote within one business day. A deposit confirms your booking and locks in your preferred date.

Still have questions? We're ready to talk through your project.
